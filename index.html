Anneer Center is an independent think tank. We study the choices that shape nations and ask, of every policy, who gains and who pays.

Named after Jabal Al-Nir, the landmark mountain of Najd, the Center is based in Saudi Arabia and global in scope. Our research spans energy, cities, technology, the economy, resources and governance.
