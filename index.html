About Anneer

Named for a mountain. Built for clear sight.

Anneer takes its name from Jabal Al-Nir, the landmark mountain of Najd. Like a summit, a good think tank should offer a long view: steady, independent and clear about what it sees.

Based in Saudi Arabia and global in scope, the Center studies the policy questions that matter most to governments, businesses and citizens, and asks how their costs and benefits are shared.

Our approach

How we work →
01

Independent

Our conclusions follow the evidence. We state our positions plainly and show the strongest arguments against them.

02

Rigorous

Every claim is sourced and every figure can be checked. We compare cases across countries before drawing conclusions.

03

Distributional

We ask not only whether a policy works, but who gains, who pays and how the balance can be made fairer.
